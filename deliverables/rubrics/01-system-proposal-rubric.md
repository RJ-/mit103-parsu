# Rubric — System Proposal

Assessed at master's level: description earns Developing, justification earns Proficient, and defensible justification against a named alternative earns Excellent.

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Problem definition** | Problem is evidenced with data, bounded, and clearly distinct from any solution. | Problem is clear and evidenced but partly framed as a solution. | Problem stated but asserted rather than evidenced. | Problem is a solution in disguise. | 20% |
| **Stakeholder and organisational analysis** | Full map with interests, influence and veto power; culture explicitly analysed. | Complete map; culture mentioned. | Main stakeholders identified; analysis thin. | List of names only. | 15% |
| **Context diagram and use cases** | Correct single-process context diagram; scenarios cover main and alternate flows. | Correct diagram; scenarios cover main flows. | Minor notation errors; scenarios incomplete. | Notation errors that change meaning. | 15% |
| **Elicitation instrument quality** | Questions are non-leading, well-sequenced, and matched to the method; JAD use justified. | Sound instruments with minor sequencing issues. | Usable instruments with some leading questions. | Leading or unusable questions. | 20% |
| **Requirements and traceability** | Every requirement traces to a named source; 29148 structure fully applied. | Most requirements traced; structure applied. | Some traceability; structure partly applied. | No traceability. | 20% |
| **Scholarly presentation** | Clean, cited, correctly attributed, professional register throughout. | Clear and cited with minor lapses. | Readable; citation inconsistent. | Poor structure or missing attribution. | 10% |

**Score** = Σ (level × weight) ÷ 4 × 100.

A criterion scored 1 anywhere means the deliverable is returned for revision before a final mark is recorded.
