# Deliverable 7 — Detailed System Proposal and Implementation Result

**Due:** Week 18 · **Assessed under:** Laboratory / Performance (40%)  
**Program outcomes:** a.5, g.3, i.3 — plus PO9 i.2 and i.3 on defence

This is the capstone output for the course. It consolidates every earlier
deliverable into a research-format document and adds implementation evidence.

## Chapters 1–5 research format

**Chapter 1 — The Problem and Its Setting.** Background, statement of the
problem, objectives, scope and delimitation, significance, definition of terms.

**Chapter 2 — Review of Related Literature and Systems.** Peer-reviewed sources
and comparable systems. At least one recent arXiv cs.SE preprint. Blog posts and
vendor marketing do not count toward the minimum.

**Chapter 3 — Methodology.** Development methodology chosen and justified
against alternatives. Elicitation methods, respondents, sampling, instruments.
Include your ethics and data privacy handling under the Data Privacy Act.

**Chapter 4 — Results and Discussion.** The analysis and design artefacts:
requirements, DFDs, ERD and data dictionary, process specifications, interface
designs. Present them as findings, with discussion — not as an appendix dump.

**Chapter 5 — Summary, Conclusions and Recommendations.** Including what you
would do differently and what a maintainer would need next.

## Implementation Result

- Working artefact or vertical slice, whatever scope you committed to.
- Test records: what you tested, how, what failed, what you fixed.
- Deployment or environment notes.
- Evaluation against all nine **ISO/IEC 25010:2023** quality characteristics.
  Name the two you have real evidence for and the two you neglected.

## Panel defence

Ten-minute presentation plus questions. You will be asked to justify one design
decision you did not expect to defend. Know your alternatives.
