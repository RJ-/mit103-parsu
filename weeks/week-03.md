# Week 03 — Understanding and Modeling Organizational Systems

**Program outcomes:** `b.3` · `d.2`  
**Portfolio thread:** System Proposal — Part 1

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week you should be able to:

- Explain that organizations and their members are systems, and that analysts must take a systems perspective.
- Depict systems graphically using context-level data flow diagrams, entity-relationship models, and use cases with use case scenarios.
- Recognize that different levels of management require different systems.
- Assess how organizational culture shapes the design of information systems.

## Instructional content

- Understanding and modeling organizational systems

## In class

- **Lecture discussion (45 min).** Systems perspective; boundaries, environment, feedback. Levels of management and the systems each demands.
- **Modelling clinic (60 min).** Working from a shared mini-case, the class builds one context-level DFD and one use case scenario together, then students redraw the pair for their own domain.
- **Culture probe (20 min).** Short discussion: name a system in your organisation that failed for cultural rather than technical reasons.

## Lab and independent work

- Install **draw.io** (desktop or browser) and **Archi**.
- Produce a context-level DFD, a stakeholder map, and two use case scenarios for your chosen domain.
- Commit source files, not just images — `.drawio` files are text and diff cleanly.

## Assessment

Individual Activity — System Proposal (Laboratory/Performance, 40%). See [System Proposal](../deliverables/01-system-proposal.md).

## Check yourself

- Your context diagram has exactly one process. If you were tempted to draw more, what were you actually modelling?
- Which external entity in your diagram has the power to veto the project? Is that visible anywhere in your model?
