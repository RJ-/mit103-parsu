# Week 01 — Course Orientation and Institutional Alignment

**Program outcomes:** `f.3`  
**Portfolio thread:** Setup — no graded portfolio output yet

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week you should be able to:

- Articulate the University's vision, mission and core values, the College goals and program objectives, and the Quality Policy.
- Relate these institutional statements to your own field of practice.
- Explain the course content, description, goals, grading policy and teaching strategies.
- Position MIT 103 within the wider Master of Information Technology program.

## Instructional content

- Vision and Mission
- Goals and Objectives

## In class

- **Course orientation (30 min).** Walk the syllabus: outcomes, the 18-week arc, and how the weekly deliverables accumulate into the final Chapters 1–5 proposal.
- **Guided discussion (30 min).** Each student names one information system they use or maintain at work, and one thing about it they would change. Capture these on the board — they become candidate problem domains.
- **Recitation (20 min).** VMGO, College goals, Quality Policy.
- **Individual presentation (40 min).** Two minutes each: *Which program outcome matters most in my current job, and why?*

## Lab and independent work

- Create a GitHub account if you do not have one, and send your username through Google Classroom.
- Fork this repository and confirm you can view the rendered site.
- Draft a one-paragraph statement of the organisational problem you may want to work on for the semester. Nothing is locked in yet.

## Assessment

Recitation and individual presentation (Class Standing, 20%).

## Check yourself

- The Quality Policy commits the University to continual improvement of its quality management system. Name one concrete way a systems analyst contributes to that.
- Which of the nine program outcomes will your final System Proposal most directly evidence?
