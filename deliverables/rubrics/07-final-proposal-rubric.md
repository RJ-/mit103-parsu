# Rubric — Detailed System Proposal and Implementation Result

The capstone rubric. Chapters 1–5 plus implementation evidence plus panel defence.

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Chapter 1 — problem and setting** | Objectives are specific and measurable; scope and delimitation honestly bounded. | Clear objectives and scope. | General objectives. | Vague or missing. | 10% |
| **Chapter 2 — literature and systems** | Peer-reviewed sources critically synthesised, not summarised; gap clearly identified. | Adequate review with some synthesis. | Sources summarised serially. | Insufficient or non-scholarly sources. | 20% |
| **Chapter 3 — methodology** | Methodology justified against named alternatives; ethics and Data Privacy Act handling explicit. | Methodology justified; ethics addressed. | Methodology described. | Methodology asserted. | 15% |
| **Chapter 4 — results and discussion** | Artefacts presented as findings with genuine discussion and integration across weeks. | Artefacts presented coherently. | Artefacts assembled with little discussion. | Appendix dump. | 20% |
| **Implementation and quality evaluation** | Working slice with test evidence; all nine ISO/IEC 25010 characteristics assessed honestly. | Working slice with test evidence and partial quality assessment. | Artefact present, evidence thin. | No working evidence. | 20% |
| **Defence** | Handles unanticipated challenge; alternatives known and weighed on the spot. | Answers confidently and accurately. | Answers rehearsed material only. | Cannot justify own decisions. | 15% |

**Score** = Σ (level × weight) ÷ 4 × 100.

A criterion scored 1 anywhere means the deliverable is returned for revision before a final mark is recorded.
