# Rubric — System Design (DFDs)

Notation correctness is a threshold, not an achievement. Marks concentrate on decomposition judgement and the logical-to-physical transition.

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Notation correctness** | Fully correct throughout; consistent numbering and naming. | One or two trivial errors. | Several errors not affecting meaning. | Errors that change meaning. | 15% |
| **Balancing and levelling** | All child diagrams balance; decomposition depth justified. | Diagrams balance. | Minor imbalances. | Unbalanced. | 20% |
| **Current vs proposed distinction** | Difference is explicit and every change is motivated by a requirement. | Difference is clear. | Difference partly visible. | Sets are near-identical. | 20% |
| **Physical DFD and partitioning** | Derived correctly; partitioning decisions justified against deployment reality. | Derived correctly with justification. | Derived with weak justification. | Not derived from the logical set. | 25% |
| **BPMN comparison** | Model is correct and the comparative note shows real analytical insight. | Model correct, note adequate. | Model correct, note superficial. | Model incorrect or note missing. | 20% |

**Score** = Σ (level × weight) ÷ 4 × 100.

A criterion scored 1 anywhere means the deliverable is returned for revision before a final mark is recorded.
