# Rubric — System Prototype

The prototype is assessed as a requirements instrument, not as a product.

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Task coverage** | Three highest-value tasks complete end to end, including failure paths. | Three tasks complete on the happy path. | Two tasks complete. | Fragmentary screens. | 25% |
| **Prototype type fit** | Type declared and justified against the elicitation goal and the alternatives. | Type declared and justified. | Type declared, weak justification. | No declaration. | 15% |
| **Backlog and sprint discipline** | Backlog estimated, prioritised, sprint committed; items are user-value statements. | Backlog populated and estimated. | Backlog populated only. | Task list with no prioritisation. | 20% |
| **Walkthrough evidence** | Real user, faithful record, surprises reported honestly. | Proxy user, sound record. | Session held, thin record. | No walkthrough. | 25% |
| **Change log** | Every change traced to a specific finding; declined changes justified. | Changes traced to findings. | Changes listed without linkage. | No change log. | 15% |

**Score** = Σ (level × weight) ÷ 4 × 100.

A criterion scored 1 anywhere means the deliverable is returned for revision before a final mark is recorded.
