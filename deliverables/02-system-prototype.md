# Deliverable 2 — System Prototype

**Due:** end of Week 6 · **Assessed under:** Laboratory / Performance (40%)  
**Program outcomes:** a.4, c.4

## What you submit

1. **Low-fidelity clickable prototype** in Penpot covering your three
   highest-value user tasks end to end.
2. **Prototype type declaration.** Which of the four types you built
   (patched-up, non-operational, first-of-a-series, selected features) and why
   that type fits your goal.
3. **Product backlog** in Taiga, populated and estimated, with one sprint
   committed. Include a board screenshot and an export.
4. **Walkthrough record.** One session with a real or proxy user: what you
   asked, what they did, what surprised you.
5. **Change log.** What the walkthrough changed in the prototype. If nothing
   changed, explain why the walkthrough was still worth running.

## Rubric

See [Prototype rubric](rubrics/02-prototype-rubric.md).

## Note on fidelity

Higher fidelity is not better here. A polished prototype invites feedback on
colour; a rough one invites feedback on flow. You want flow feedback this week.
