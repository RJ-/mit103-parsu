# Rubric — System Physical Design

Covers Week 10 logical design and Weeks 11–13 physical design.

| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Process specifications** | All primitives specified; notation choice justified per process; decision tables complete. | All primitives specified with justification. | Most primitives specified. | Sparse or unjustified. | 20% |
| **Output design** | Purpose, audience and medium all reasoned; output bias identified and addressed. | Sound output design with stated purpose. | Output design present, purpose implicit. | Decorative output with no stated purpose. | 20% |
| **Input design** | Applies named GOV.UK or USWDS patterns; derives what it can rather than asking. | Applies established patterns. | Functional forms, ad hoc patterns. | Forms replicate existing problems. | 20% |
| **Responsive and cross-platform** | Full set; what was dropped at each breakpoint is stated and justified. | Full set with notes. | Two platforms only. | Single platform. | 20% |
| **Accessibility** | WCAG 2.2 checked, failures recorded, fixes applied and re-verified. | WCAG checked and mostly addressed. | Contrast checked only. | Not checked. | 20% |

**Score** = Σ (level × weight) ÷ 4 × 100.

A criterion scored 1 anywhere means the deliverable is returned for revision before a final mark is recorded.
