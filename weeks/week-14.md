# Week 14 — Introduction to Human-Computer Interaction

**Program outcomes:** `h.2` · `b.4`  
**Portfolio thread:** System Interface Design

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week you should be able to:

- Explain the concept and importance of human-computer interaction.
- Recognize the role of HCI in designing user-centered systems.
- Design useful touch screen interfaces for smartphones and tablets.

## Instructional content

- Introduction to Human-Computer Interaction
- Importance of HCI in systems design
- Principles of user-centered interaction design
- Designing touch screen interfaces for smartphones and tablets

## In class

- **Lecture-discussion (35 min).** Human capabilities and limits; why HCI is an engineering constraint, not a finishing layer.
- **Interface analysis (50 min).** Teardown of one widely used Philippine government or banking app against user-centred principles.
- **Guided discussion (25 min).** Touch-specific design: target size, gesture discoverability, reachability.

## Lab and independent work

- Produce a touch interface for the two most frequent tasks in your system.
- Document your user-centred design process against the ISO 9241-210 activity cycle.

## Assessment

Initial System Physical Design output; recitation and rubric-based assessment.

## Check yourself

- Which of your interactions requires precision your users will not have on a bumpy jeepney ride?
- Where in your process did a real user's input change a design decision?
