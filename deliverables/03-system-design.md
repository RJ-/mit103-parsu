# Deliverable 3 — System Design (Data Flow Diagrams)

**Due:** end of Week 7 · **Assessed under:** Laboratory / Performance (40%)  
**Program outcomes:** a.3, d.2

## What you submit

1. **Current system:** context diagram, Level-0, at least one exploded Level-1.
2. **Proposed system:** context diagram, Level-0, at least one exploded Level-1.
3. **One physical DFD** derived from your logical set, with partitioning shown
   and partitioning decisions justified.
4. **One BPMN model** of a high-value process, built in bpmn.io.
5. **Comparative note** (150 words): what BPMN captured that the DFD could not,
   and vice versa.
6. **Balance check statement.** Confirm, flow by flow, that child diagrams
   balance against their parents.

## Rubric

See [System Design rubric](rubrics/03-system-design-rubric.md).

## Non-negotiables

- Every process is numbered and every number follows the parent-child scheme.
- Every data flow is named with a noun phrase, not a verb.
- No process has only inputs or only outputs.
- Diagram sources are committed, not just PNGs.
