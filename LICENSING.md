### Specific cautions

- **SWEBOK v4.0, OMG specifications (UML, BPMN), ISO previews,
  softengbook.org, and the IxDF Encyclopedia** are free to read but **not**
  openly licensed. Link, never upload.
- **NonCommercial (NC) licences** permit classroom use at a public university.
  They do not permit resale or inclusion in paid training products.
- **NoDerivatives (ND)** — note that *Introduction to Human-Computer Interaction*
  is CC BY-NC-ND. You may distribute it unchanged; you may not create an adapted
  version. Assign chapters as-is.
- **ISO and IEEE standards themselves are paid.** The free substitutes here
  (SEBoK articles, ReqView templates, ISO Online Browsing Platform previews) are
  sufficient for teaching, but **cite the standard number** in student
  deliverables regardless of which substitute you read.

## Data privacy

Student work in this repository may involve real organisational data. Under the
Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), personal data must be anonymised
before it enters a public repository. This applies to interview transcripts,
sample records, screenshots containing names, and system exports.

If you discover personal data in a commit, tell the instructor immediately.
Deleting the file does not remove it from Git history — the commit must be
rewritten or the repository purged.
