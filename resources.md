# Open and free resource list

40 curated resources · 6 categories · all 18 weeks mapped · free to access

## How to read this list

Every entry is free to access. They are **not** all free to reuse, so each
carries one of three badges.

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span> | Openly licensed (CC BY / CC BY-SA / CC BY-NC). Free to reuse, remix and redistribute with attribution. |
| <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span> | Free of charge to read and download, but **not** openly licensed. Do not re-host or remix — link to the source. |
| <span class="badge badge-oss">OSS</span> | Open-source software under an OSI-approved licence (GPL, MPL, Apache, EPL, MIT). |

## A. Core open textbooks — information systems, analysis and design

**1. Foundations of Information Systems** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
M. S. Raisinghani (ed.), OpenStax / Rice University, 2025 · CC BY-NC-SA 4.0  
Newest peer-reviewed IS textbook. IS fundamentals, data management, databases,
systems development, security, emerging tech. Instructor slides and test banks
available.  
→ [openstax.org/details/books/foundations-information-systems](https://openstax.org/details/books/foundations-information-systems)

**2. Information Systems for Business and Beyond (2019 ed.)** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Bourgeois, Smith, Wang & Mortati, Saylor / Biola, 2019 · CC BY-NC 4.0  
The most widely adopted IS open textbook. Ch. 10 maps directly to Week 2.  
→ [opentextbook.site/exports/ISBB-2019.pdf](https://opentextbook.site/exports/ISBB-2019.pdf) (direct PDF)

**3. Information Systems for Business and Beyond — Canadian Edition** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Ramim et al., eCampusOntario Pressbooks · CC BY-NC  
Non-US cases; useful for contrasting regulatory contexts in Week 3.  
→ [ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/informationsystemscdn](https://ecampusontario.pressbooks.pub/informationsystemscdn)

**4. Business Information Systems: Design an App for That** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Open Textbook Library / Saylor Foundation · CC BY  
Project-based, organised around the full SDLC. Strong fit for the running
proposal/prototype portfolio.  
→ [open.umn.edu](https://open.umn.edu/opentextbooks/textbooks/business-information-systems-design-an-app-for-that)

**5. Database Design — 2nd edition** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Watt & Eng, BCcampus Open Education · CC BY 4.0  
142 pages: data models, ER modelling, functional dependency, normalisation, SQL.
ERD exercises and a full SQL lab with solutions. Week 8 anchor.  
→ [opentextbc.ca/dbdesign01](https://opentextbc.ca/dbdesign01)

## B. Software and requirements engineering

**6. Requirements Engineering** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Regnell (ed.), Lund University · CC BY-SA 4.0  
LaTeX-source textbook plus ready-made slide decks in `/slides` and `/lectures`.
Reusable for Weeks 4–5.  
→ [github.com/bjornregnell/reqeng-book](https://github.com/bjornregnell/reqeng-book)

**7. Handbook of Software Engineering Methods — 2nd edition** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Letaw, Oregon State University, 2024 · CC BY-NC 4.0  
Method-by-method: user stories, personas, inclusive design (GenderMag), sprint
planning, code review, usability testing.  
→ [open.oregonstate.education/setextbook](https://open.oregonstate.education/setextbook)

**8. SWEBOK v4.0** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Washizaki (ed.), IEEE Computer Society, 2024 · free for personal and academic use  
18 knowledge areas. Use KA 1 (Requirements), KA 2 (Architecture), KA 3 (Design),
KA 8 (Quality) as graduate-level anchors.  
→ [swebok-v4.pdf](https://ieeecs-media.computer.org/media/education/swebok/swebok-v4.pdf) (direct PDF)

**9. SEBoK — Systems Engineering Body of Knowledge** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
INCOSE / IEEE / Stevens Institute · CC BY-NC-SA 3.0  
Citable living wiki. Its ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 article is the best legal substitute
for the paywalled standard.  
→ [sebokwiki.org](https://sebokwiki.org)

**10. Software Engineering: A Modern Approach** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Valente, 2024 · free web version, all rights reserved  
900+ instructor slides available on request. **Link to it — do not re-host.**  
→ [softengbook.org](https://softengbook.org)

**11. Software Engineering: Standing on the Shoulders of Giants** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
SWEBook project · Creative Commons  
Built around landmark SE papers — seed material for graduate critique (PO4 d.1).  
→ [swebook.org](https://swebook.org)

## C. Modelling notations and standards

**12. OMG UML 2.5.1** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Object Management Group, 2017 · free for informational use  
Normative source for use case, class, sequence and activity diagrams. Cite this
rather than a textbook redraw when defending models.  
→ [omg.org/spec/UML/2.5.1/PDF](https://www.omg.org/spec/UML/2.5.1/PDF)

**13. OMG BPMN 2.0.2 / ISO 19510** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Enterprise process analysis in Weeks 3 and 7, alongside classic DFDs.  
→ [omg.org/spec/BPMN/2.0.2/PDF](https://www.omg.org/spec/BPMN/2.0.2/PDF)

**14. The Scrum Guide (Nov 2020)** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Schwaber & Sutherland · CC BY-SA 4.0  
13 pages, definitive. Available in 30+ languages including Filipino.  
→ [scrumguides.org](https://scrumguides.org/docs/scrumguide/v2020/2020-Scrum-Guide-US.pdf) (direct PDF)

**15. Manifesto for Agile Software Development** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Beck, Cockburn, Fowler et al., 2001 · free to reproduce in full  
Primary source for Week 6.  
→ [agilemanifesto.org](https://agilemanifesto.org)

**16. ISO/IEC 25010:2023 — Product Quality Model (SQuaRE)** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Read-only preview free; full text paid  
Nine quality characteristics. Note: *Usability* became *Interaction Capability*
in the 2023 revision. Week 18 criteria.  
→ [iso.org/obp/ui](https://www.iso.org/obp/ui) (free preview)

**17. ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 requirements templates** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
ReqView templates + SEBoK article · templates free, standard paid  
Free SRS / StRS / SyRS templates conforming to 29148:2018.  
→ [reqview.com/doc/iso-iec-ieee-29148-templates](https://www.reqview.com/doc/iso-iec-ieee-29148-templates)

## D. Human-computer interaction, interface and input/output design

**18. Introduction to Human-Computer Interaction** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
Hornbæk, Kristensson & Oulasvirta, OUP, 2025 · CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 open access  
864 pages, 46 chapters, 200+ principles. Free chapter-level PDFs. **Anchors
Weeks 11–15.**  
→ [academic.oup.com/book/60808](https://academic.oup.com/book/60808)

**19. The Encyclopedia of Human-Computer Interaction, 2nd ed.** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Soegaard & Dam (eds.), Interaction Design Foundation · free to read online  
4,000+ pages with chapters by Norman, Shneiderman, Card. Good for assigning
individual chapters as seminar readings.  
→ [interaction-design.org](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/book/the-encyclopedia-of-human-computer-interaction-2nd-ed)

**20. 10 Usability Heuristics for User Interface Design** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Nielsen, NN/g (rev. 2024) · free article and poster  
The standard heuristic-evaluation instrument. Becomes the Week 15 critique rubric.  
→ [nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics)

**21. WCAG 2.2** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
W3C Recommendation, 5 October 2023 · W3C Document Licence  
Normative accessibility criteria for the input/output weeks. Pair with the
*How to Meet WCAG* quick reference for a checkable rubric.  
→ [w3.org/TR/WCAG22](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22)

**22. GOV.UK Design System and Service Manual** <span class="badge badge-open">OPEN</span>  
UK Government Digital Service · MIT / Open Government Licence  
Production-grade, evidence-backed patterns for forms, error messages and
question pages — exactly the Weeks 12–13 and 16–17 content, with research
citations behind every pattern.  
→ [design-system.service.gov.uk](https://design-system.service.gov.uk)

**23. U.S. Web Design System (USWDS)** <span class="badge badge-oss">OSS</span>  
U.S. General Services Administration · public domain (CC0) / open source  
Accessible component library plus detailed form and validation guidance. Code
students can actually build prototypes on.  
→ [designsystem.digital.gov](https://designsystem.digital.gov)

**24. Material Design 3** <span class="badge badge-free">FREE</span>  
Google · Apache 2.0 (code); free guidelines  
Dashboards, widgets, adaptive layouts, touch and tablet interfaces. Weeks 11 and 14.  
→ [m3.material.io](https://m3.material.io)
