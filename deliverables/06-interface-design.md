# Deliverable 6 — System Interface Design

**Due:** end of Week 17 · **Assessed under:** Laboratory / Performance (40%)  
**Weeks covered:** 14, 15, 16, 17 · **Program outcomes:** a.2, a.3, a.5, b.4, f.2, g.1, h.2

## What you submit

1. **Touch interface** for the two most frequent tasks in your system.
2. **User-centred design process record**, mapped to the ISO 9241-210 activity
   cycle. Show where real user input changed a decision.
3. **Heuristic evaluation received.** The two evaluations classmates performed on
   your prototype in Week 15, with severity ratings.
4. **Response to findings.** Every severity-3 and severity-4 finding either fixed
   or explicitly declined with justification.
5. **Onscreen dialog set.** Error, confirmation, empty-state and success
   messages. Each says what happened and what to do next; verbs stay consistent
   from button to confirmation.
6. **Coding scheme** for the system, documented in the data dictionary, with a
   note on what facts you deliberately did not embed in codes.
7. **Data capture specification** per entity: human, automated, or integrated.
8. **Validation specification**, tied to the data dictionary, covering client,
   server, database and business-process layers.
9. **Validation message set**, checked against WCAG 2.2 criteria 3.3.1–3.3.6.
