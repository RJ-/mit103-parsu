# Deliverable 4 — Database Design and Data Dictionary

**Due:** end of Week 8 · **Assessed under:** Laboratory / Performance (40%)  
**Program outcomes:** a.2, b.4

## What you submit

1. **Entity-relationship diagram**, normalised to at least 3NF, built in
   pgModeler.
2. **Normalisation trace.** Show the progression from unnormalised through 1NF,
   2NF, 3NF for at least two non-trivial relations.
3. **Denormalisation justification**, if any. State the performance or usability
   argument and the integrity risk you accept.
4. **Data dictionary** covering every data store and data flow in your Level-0
   DFD, using the [template](../templates/data-dictionary.md). Each entry needs
   name, type, domain, source, and the process that creates it.
5. **DDL script** generated from pgModeler, committed as `.sql`.
6. **Repository note** (150 words): what a CASE repository gives you that a
   document does not.
