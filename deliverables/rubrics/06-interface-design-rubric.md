# Rubric — System Interface Design



| Criterion | Excellent (4) | Proficient (3) | Developing (2) | Beginning (1) | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **HCI application** | User-centred process evidenced; real user input demonstrably changed a decision. | Process followed and documented. | Process claimed, evidence thin. | No process evidence. | 20% |
| **Response to heuristic evaluation** | All severity 3–4 findings resolved or declined with a defensible argument. | Most findings resolved. | Some findings resolved. | Findings ignored. | 20% |
| **Onscreen dialog** | Messages state what happened and what to do next; verbs consistent across the flow. | Clear messages; minor inconsistency. | Messages present but vague. | System-voice or missing messages. | 15% |
| **Coding and data capture** | Scheme is stable, documented, and deliberately avoids embedding volatile facts. | Scheme documented and workable. | Scheme present, undocumented. | Ad hoc. | 20% |
| **Validation specification** | All four layers addressed; rules tied to the dictionary; messages meet WCAG 3.3.x. | Multiple layers addressed and tied to the dictionary. | Client-side rules only. | Incomplete. | 25% |

**Score** = Σ (level × weight) ÷ 4 × 100.

A criterion scored 1 anywhere means the deliverable is returned for revision before a final mark is recorded.
