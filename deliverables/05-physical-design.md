# Deliverable 5 — System Physical Design

**Due:** end of Week 13 · **Assessed under:** Laboratory / Performance (40%)  
**Weeks covered:** 10 (logical design), 11, 12, 13 · **Program outcomes:** a.3, a.4, d.2, g.1, h.2, i.1

## What you submit

### Part A — Logical design (from Week 10)

1. **Process specifications** for every primitive process in your Level-1 DFD,
   using structured English, decision tables or decision trees.
2. **Method justification** for each specification. Why this notation for this
   process.
3. **One PlantUML activity diagram** committed as source code.
4. **Output evaluation** (1 page): an existing output from your organisation
   assessed against effective-output-design criteria, including any output bias.

### Part B — Physical design (Weeks 11–13)

5. **Primary output screen** at medium fidelity in Penpot.
6. **Dashboard design**, with a stated decision it supports.
7. **Input forms** for the core transactions.
8. **Responsive set:** desktop, tablet, phone variants, with a note on what was
   dropped at each step down and why.
9. **Accessibility record.** WCAG 2.2 contrast and structure check results,
   failures found, fixes applied.
10. **Redesign case:** one real institutional form, before and after, annotated
    against GOV.UK or USWDS patterns.
