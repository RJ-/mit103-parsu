# Week 05 — Information Gathering: Unobtrusive Methods

**Program outcomes:** `b.4` · `d.2`  
**Portfolio thread:** System Proposal — requirements consolidation

## Learning outcomes

By the end of this week you should be able to:

- Recognize the value of unobtrusive methods for information gathering.
- Explain sampling for human information requirements analysis.
- Construct useful samples of people, documents and events.
- Create an analyst's playscript to observe decision-maker activities.
- Apply the STROBE technique to observe and interpret the decision maker's environment and interaction with technologies.

## In class

- **Lecture discussion (30 min).** Sampling frames; document analysis; the limits of what people can tell you about their own work.
- **Interactive learning — STROBE exercise (50 min).** Students analyse photographs of three real workspaces and infer decision-making style, then defend the inference. Disagreement is the point.
- **Playscript drafting (40 min).** Convert one observed process into an analyst's playscript, then compare against the interview data gathered in Week 4. Where do the two disagree?

## Lab and independent work

- Perform a document/artefact analysis on real forms, reports or screens from your domain.
- Consolidate Weeks 3–5 evidence into a first **SRS draft** using the 29148 template. Every requirement must trace to a source (interview, document or observation).
- Submit the System Proposal package as a pull request.

## Assessment

System Proposal (Laboratory/Performance, 40%) — first major graded submission.

## Check yourself

- Name one requirement you would never have discovered through interviews alone.
- Your sample of documents — is it representative, or just the documents that were easy to obtain?
