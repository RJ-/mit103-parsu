organisational problem in Week 1–2 and carry it through to the Chapters 1–5
proposal in Week 18. Nothing here is a standalone exercise.

## Grading context

From the syllabus, for laboratory classes:

| Component | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Laboratory / performance skills, outputs, projects | 40% |
| Midterm / Final examination | 30% |
| Class standing (quizzes and recitation) | 20% |
| Deportment / attendance | 10% |

`FINAL GRADE = (final term grade × 50%) + (midterm grade × 50%)`

## How to submit

All deliverables are submitted as a pull request to `submissions/<your-surname>/`
in this repository, and the PR link is posted to Google Classroom by the deadline.
Google Classroom holds the official timestamp. See [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Standing expectations at master's level

- **Traceability.** Every design decision traces to a requirement; every
  requirement traces to an elicitation source. Untraceable decisions lose marks
  regardless of how good they look.
- **Justification over description.** Saying what you built earns little. Saying
  why, against what alternative, earns the marks.
- **Citation.** Use the open resources in [resources](../resources.md). Cite
  standards by number even when you accessed a free substitute.
- **Real data, real constraints.** Where possible, work on a genuine problem in
  your own organisation. Anonymise where required by the Data Privacy Act.
