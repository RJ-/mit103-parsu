# Deliverable 1 — System Proposal

**Due:** end of Week 5 · **Assessed under:** Laboratory / Performance (40%)  
**Weeks covered:** 3, 4, 5 · **Program outcomes:** b.3, b.4, d.2, f.3

## What you submit

1. **Organisational context** (2–3 pages). The organisation, its structure, and
   the levels of management the system will serve.
2. **Problem statement.** What is failing now, with evidence, not assertion.
3. **Stakeholder map.** Roles, interests, influence. Mark who can veto.
4. **Context-level data flow diagram.** One process, all external entities.
5. **Two use case scenarios**, written out in full.
6. **Elicitation instruments.** Your interview guide and questionnaire, plus a
   note on how each was structured and why.
7. **Elicitation evidence.** Interview notes, document analysis, STROBE
   observations. Anonymised.
8. **Requirements set** in the ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148 SRS template, with each
   requirement traced to its source.
9. **Feasibility assessment.** Technical, operational, economic, schedule.

## Format

Markdown or PDF in `submissions/<surname>/01-proposal/`. Diagram source files
(`.drawio`, `.puml`) committed alongside exported images.

## Rubric

See [System Proposal rubric](rubrics/01-system-proposal-rubric.md).

## Common failure modes

- A problem statement that is really a solution statement in disguise.
- Requirements with no traceable source — you decided them, nobody told you.
- A context diagram with more than one process in the middle.
- Interview questions that could only produce the answer you wanted.
